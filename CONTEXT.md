# ComplianceHub domain

ComplianceHub coordinates a company's ongoing security and compliance work. A saved baseline records a starting or later position within that lifecycle; it does not define the whole product.

## Language

**Workspace**: A company's separately governed collection of compliance records and members.
_Avoid_: treating a person's account as the workspace.

**Compliance coordinator**: A person responsible for organising the programme and following up its work. This responsibility does not itself grant access or approval rights.

**Accountable contributor**: The person currently assigned to a task, responsible for supplying their work and its limitations.
_Avoid_: treating assignment as permission to change every compliance record.

**Employee obligation**: Work an employee must fulfil, such as acknowledging the applicable version of a policy. Acknowledgement is distinct from approving the policy.

**Authorised reviewer**: A person permitted to make a particular review decision. Review authority is specific to the workflow; it is not implied by a job title.

**Scope**: The recorded organisational boundary, services, information, dependencies and exclusions to which the programme applies.

**Assessment**: Answers and supporting notes against a defined set of questions used to identify the starting position and gaps.

**Control decision**: The recorded applicability, implementation state, rationale and accountability for a security control.
_Avoid_: certification, verified effectiveness.

**Risk**: A recorded exposure with likelihood, impact, treatment and an accountable decision about residual exposure.

**Task completion**: A recorded decision that an assigned unit of work is done. It does not independently establish evidence freshness, successful technical resolution or finding closure.

**Contribution**: A person's preserved submission describing work, sources and limitations for review.

**Human review**: A person's recorded decision about specified work or evidence. Accepting a contribution preserves that exact note as linked evidence; it does not complete its task or verify a live system.

**Evidence freshness**: Whether a record remains within its recorded validity period and has not been withdrawn or superseded. Freshness is distinct from quality, human acceptance and provider verification.

**Provider verification**: A recorded result from an actual connected system check. Proposals, sandbox samples and completed tasks are not provider verification.

**Finding closure**: An explicit recorded decision that an audit or monitoring finding is resolved under its own workflow. Completing corrective work alone is not closure.

**Dated baseline**: A preserved account of scope, assessment, work, evidence and risks at a particular save. Comparisons require compatible recorded scope and calculation basis.
_Avoid_: current live state, leadership approval.

**Leadership decision**: An authorised person's explicit decision about priorities, resources or accepted exposure. Viewing a report or reading an AI suggestion does not record such a decision.
